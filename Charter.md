This is a test project to research a hypothetical. BMW offers an iPhone app for doing things to your vehicle, like turning on the climate controls in preparation for departure, unlocking, etc. There used to be an API that this could be done through but it seems to no longer be available. The app exists so the ability to do these things remotely has not gone away.

Research this problem and verify the current state of affairs. Then look at potential solutions. Our goal is to have an API we can use for our own app or integration into other systems such as home assistant.

You may explore sample code, preferably swift, but I do not have access to an actual BMW to test with.
